About

FlyingRat by MBR is a personal electric vehicle (PEV) repair and parts business based in Singapore. Founded by Goh Kong Wee, we specialize in electric scooters, electric unicycles (EUCs), and other personal mobility devices.

With a background in motor mechanics, we understand how things work from the ground up. We are passionate about keeping riders safe and their vehicles running at peak performance. We are also exploring how technology, blockchain, and AI can improve the PEV community.
